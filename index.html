Residents born in post-Soviet countries

The Census Bureau’s B05006 table reports birthplace of the foreign-born population, not ethnicity or ancestry. Choose up to three countries or regions from the menu, or click the legend to show and hide lines. Hover over a point for its estimate and margin of error (MOE). Regional totals combine countries that B05006 reports separately in each period, so earlier totals may not include every country. Combined regional MOEs are approximate.
